# ITNL liability aggregate review

The original FY2015, FY2016 and FY2017 standalone balance sheets have been rendered and reviewed. Source hashes and URLs are pinned in `configs/sources.yaml`; reviewed PDF pages are 116, 114 and 114 respectively.

Total liabilities are now an explicit sum of the printed non-current and current liability subtotals. Earlier statements interleave each subtotal with its final provision line; reviewed column positions select those printed subtotals. The later Ind AS statement labels both totals directly. No missing components are inferred, and this method is applied separately within each source year, accounting basis and denomination.

The FY2017 standalone sheet separately identifies long-term and current borrowings, plus narrow cash and cash equivalents. These explicitly printed amounts are mapped; other bank balances and unknown short-term investment components are not substituted for the broader cash input. Current maturities within other financial liabilities remain unresolved, so a total-debt aggregate is not guessed.

Original and later restated comparatives keep their own publication dates. The FY2017 opening transition column is not relabelled as a prior financial year. Missing inventory, retained earnings and other unreviewed inputs remain explicit; this aggregate review does not complete the nonfinancial history gate.
