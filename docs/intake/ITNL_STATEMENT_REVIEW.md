# ITNL original-statement review

Reviewed on 2026-10-29. Exact report hashes, source URLs, units, row captions and year positions are pinned in `configs/sources.yaml`. All imported observations retain their source pages and conservative annual-report publication dates; later restatements retain later availability.

The original FY2015 report's balance sheet and reserve notes are on PDF pages 116 and 128–129. Earned reserves are the closing general reserve, debenture redemption reserve and profit-and-loss surplus. Securities premium and currency/hedging reserves are excluded. The FY2016 reserve note is on PDF page 127. Its repeated captions refer to distinct printed reserve rows; the extractor now identifies physical cells rather than rejecting every repeated caption.

The FY2017 statement of changes in equity is on PDF page 118. Its earned-reserve balance is the printed total excluding securities premium. Date numerals before the amount columns are explicitly accounted for. The Ind AS comparative is retained as a later published observation, without replacing earlier GAAP values. Cross-standard lag comparisons remain a documented limitation.

FY2015 and FY2014 inventory is an exact residual of the original FY2015 balance sheet: the printed current-assets subtotal less every printed component of that complete section. Both residuals reconcile to zero at source precision. This is not a conversion of a dash to zero. A later report's comparative inventory dash remains missing. Later cash/other-asset reclassifications retain their own published vintages.
