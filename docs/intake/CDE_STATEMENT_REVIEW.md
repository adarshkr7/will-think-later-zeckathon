# Coffee Day Enterprises statement review

Original receipt-protected FY2016, FY2017 and FY2018 reports are mapped in `configs/sources.yaml`. The denomination is INR million. Balance sheets are on PDF pages 100-101, 89 and 93-94 respectively; income statements are on pages 102, 90 and 95. Earned deficits are separately identified in the FY2016 reserve note on page 114 and subsequent statements of changes in equity on pages 92 and 99. Securities premium and OCI are excluded from retained earnings.

The FY2017 Ind AS comparative differs from the FY2016 original. The FY2018 report also restates the FY2017 comparative, including the merger-related share issuance. Each vintage retains the publication proxy of its own report; original observations are preserved. Opening transition balances are excluded. Audit signing dates do not establish dissemination: the configured annual-report lag is applied with imputation flagged.

For each current reporting year only, inventory is the exactly reconciled residual of the complete current-assets table after all separately printed non-inventory components. The residual equals zero at the reported precision. This is supported by the CARO assertion that there is no physical inventory at year end, on PDF pages 96, 86 and 89 respectively. Context assertions are checked independently of numerical rows and all pages remain in the source chain. No missing dash cell is converted to zero. The absence assertion is not backdated to comparative years. This source-specific reconciliation does not authorize zero inventory for ITNL or other companies.

Finance costs retain their broad source caption; they do not become interest-only expense. Purchases of stock in trade are preserved as the separately reported cost caption. The standalone data describes the holding company and does not substitute the operating subsidiaries' consolidated scale.

An isolated audit against the previously committed manual inputs plus these reviewed observations confirms three complete core annual periods before the human event date.
