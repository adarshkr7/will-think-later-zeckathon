# Exchange-session calendar source review

The original NSE Factbooks 2015 and 2018 and SEBI's Handbook of Statistics 2023?2024 are cached with immutable receipts. NSE cash-market Table 4-1 is on PDF page 40 of the 2015 Factbook and page 43 of the 2018 Factbook. SEBI's published table index links the original annual cash-market workbook; its `17A` sheet identifies NSE in S2, trading-day counts in column V and fiscal labels in column R. Exact source bytes, cells, columns, period labels and headings are pinned in `configs/calendar.yaml`. Currency/futures counts and BSE columns are separate and are not substituted for NSE equity cash counts.

The importer retains every separately published claim. Different claims for the same fiscal interval must agree before certification. Publication proxies use the time at which the raw source was publicly observed and cached, with `date_is_imputed=1`; they do not assert an earlier publication time. This calendar is an acquisition-completeness audit. It does not enter historical risk-feature snapshots.

Certification uses a complete-census proof. Let S be the true equity cash trading-day set for the exact reported fiscal interval. Each receipt-checked original production bhavcopy, with matching dates and positive traded quantities, proves a member of S. These distinct positive sessions form K, a subset of S. When a separate published exact total proves |S| = |K|, K = S; its civil-date complement is then known to be non-trading. This uses an independently checked total and positive source records, rather than treating a failed request as a closed exchange. No holiday name is inferred.

If a source count is missing, disagrees with another source, exceeds the recovered observations or is smaller than the observed count, no closed dates are certified. The output retains only positively proved trading dates until the discrepancy is resolved. Recovery checks original archives, including weekend sessions, and continues to respect the cached downloader's rate limit, retry and block rules. A negative 404 receipt records only a request outcome.

Every fiscal census has an immutable content-addressed JSON receipt under `outputs/session_census/`, listing original archive URLs, hashes, row counts and all source-count claims. Calendar rows link to their census and count sources. `derivation_method` identifies rebuildable generated rows; human entries are preserved and conflicting rows fail. Rebuilding an incomplete/conflicted interval removes stale generated closure claims. The coverage result is `outputs/session_coverage.csv`.

First-equity-listing certificates are audited separately. RHFL's original FY2018 report explicitly identifies its NSE/BSE listing on PDF page 6. Its historical listing date is retained separately from the report's later publication proxy. A confirmed pre-listing interval can explain unavailable quotes, while `full_window_quote_history` remains false. Before listing, market features remain unavailable and the company remains in the evaluation denominator.
